A simple way to encode text.

Encode Unicode text as Base64 or decode it back to readable text.

A little guidance

  1. Choose Encode or Decode.
  2. Paste plain text or a Base64 value.
  3. Run the conversion and copy the result.

Good to know

This tool supports UTF-8 text, including emoji. Base64 is an encoding, not encryption, and does not protect sensitive information.
